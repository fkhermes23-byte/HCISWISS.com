# HCI Swiss — Public Project Information

**FK HERMES INTERNATIONAL** is the public-facing umbrella brand for HCI Swiss.

HCI Swiss develops non-clinical digital operations and documentation workflows for care-service organisations and independent professionals.

This repository is intended only for public commercial information. Proprietary application code, deployment configuration, customer information, credentials and confidential operating material must not be committed here.

## Commercial enquiries

New commercial relationships are qualified for a recurring PRE / DURING / POST service model rather than isolated one-time sales. Subject to confirmed scope, eligibility, operating capacity and commercial terms, pathways may include:

- recurring CareOps workflow and documentation services;
- monthly or annual enterprise implementation and operations retainers;
- continuing integration, distribution and licensing relationships;
- ongoing monitoring, maintenance, reporting, support, renewal and separately accepted expansion.

Every arrangement must identify the actual contracting and invoicing entity and define the service, price, currency, billing frequency, renewal, cancellation or revocation path, data obligations and acceptance criteria in versioned written terms. Material changes require new informed acceptance.

Commercial information is not proof that a product or service is available for purchase in every country. The public brand is not itself a separate legal person.
