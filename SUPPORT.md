# Support, sponsorship and commercial collaboration

HCI Swiss welcomes qualified conversations with organisations, institutions, funders, sponsors, distribution partners and implementation partners whose objectives align with responsible non-clinical care operations.

## Available pathways

- Sponsor a defined public-interest or operational milestone
- Fund a scoped implementation or validation project
- Discuss institutional, distribution or integration partnerships
- Commission a CareOps workflow review
- Explore licensing after technical and commercial qualification

## Qualification

Every proposal is reviewed for scope, capacity, legal entity, geography, privacy, security, delivery obligations and applicable commercial terms.

No public statement on this repository constitutes an invoice, investment offer, charitable status, guaranteed return, universal product availability or acceptance of funds.

## Start here

Use the official website to request qualification:

https://www.hciswiss.com

The contracting or invoicing entity, deliverables, price, payment instructions and acceptance criteria must be confirmed in writing before funds are requested or accepted.
