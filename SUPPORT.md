# Recurring support and commercial collaboration

HCI Swiss welcomes qualified conversations with organisations, institutions, funders, distribution partners and implementation partners whose objectives align with responsible non-clinical care operations.

## Recurring pathways

New commercial relationships are structured as continuing services rather than isolated one-time sales. Subject to qualification, the available pathways are:

- recurring institutional or programme-support agreements tied to defined ongoing outcomes;
- monthly or annual implementation, validation and operations retainers;
- continuing distribution, integration or licensing relationships;
- recurring CareOps workflow, documentation and operational-review services;
- post-implementation maintenance, monitoring, reporting, support and renewal.

Every arrangement must define the contracting entity, service scope, delivery capacity, price, currency, billing frequency, renewal, cancellation, revocation, data obligations and acceptance criteria in versioned written terms. Material changes require a new informed acceptance.

## Qualification and payment boundary

Every proposal is reviewed for scope, capacity, legal entity, geography, privacy, security, delivery obligations and applicable commercial terms.

No public statement on this repository constitutes an invoice, investment offer, charitable status, guaranteed return, universal product availability, acceptance of funds or authority for an automatic debit. Payment collection may begin only after the applicable agreement and payment mandate have been expressly accepted and the customer has an operational cancellation or revocation path.
