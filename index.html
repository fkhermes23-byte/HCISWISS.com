Recurring service architecture

One governed relationship across the full operating lifecycle.

PRE · Readiness retainer

Recurring qualification, workflow mapping, documentation readiness, risk review and prioritisation before activation, maintained as conditions and requirements change.

DURING · Managed operations

Monthly or annual implementation, configuration, operational support and licensed use within an agreed scope, capacity model and measurable service level.

POST · Continuity & renewal

Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, reporting, support, renewal and separately accepted expansion, with an operational cancellation or revocation path.

Engagement model

A controlled path from qualification to recurring delivery.

Qualification. Confirm the organisation, problem, geography, authority, capacity and intended continuing outcome.
Versioned terms and mandate. Define service, responsibilities, price, currency, billing frequency, renewal, data obligations, cancellation and revocation before any payment authority is used.
Activation. Start only the approved scope after readiness, privacy, security and delivery controls have passed.
Continuous delivery. Measure service, document performance, provide support and reconcile agreed outputs throughout the term.
Renewal, change or cancellation. Renew transparently; obtain new informed acceptance for material changes; honour the agreed cancellation or revocation path.
